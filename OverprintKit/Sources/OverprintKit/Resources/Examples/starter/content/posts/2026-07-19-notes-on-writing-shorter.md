This one is unfinished. It exists so you can see what a draft looks like in the sidebar:
hollow dot, small tag, sitting in the list with everything else. It shows up in local
preview and nowhere else.

Notes so far, in the order they occurred to me:

- The first draft is always long because I am explaining the topic to myself. The second
  draft is where I take that scaffolding down. I keep forgetting this and treating the
  first draft as a failure.
- Cutting adjectives is the cheap version of the advice. The expensive version is cutting
  the paragraph that exists only to prove I did the reading.
- Somewhere there is a piece about the difference between short and terse. Short is
  finished. Terse is short and unfriendly. I do not have the example that shows this yet.
- Ask of every sentence whether the piece survives without it. Most of them, it does.
