I am Tessa Lindqvist. I copyedit technical documentation for a living, mostly for
companies that have just discovered their docs are eight years out of date, and I keep
Field Notes on the side. The entries are about sentences: the ones I cut, the ones I
couldn't fix, and the small rules I've collected from arguing about them for eleven
years.

Most of it starts as a note typed on my phone on the 14 tram, gets rewritten twice
in a text editor, and ends up here a week later at about half the length.

Field Notes is deliberately small: a folder, some Markdown files, and a build step that
turns them into a website. I don't run analytics on it and I'd rather not know how many
people bounce.

This site is also the starter example for Overprint, the app I write it in. If you
opened this folder to learn how Overprint works, start with the welcome post and
read forward. The three published posts cover the folder layout, the five fields at
the top of every file, and how drafts behave.

The best sentence I cut last year was eleven words long and did nothing. I think about it
more than I should.
