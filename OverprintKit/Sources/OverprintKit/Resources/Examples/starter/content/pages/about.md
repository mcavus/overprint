I am Tessa Lindqvist. I copyedit technical documentation for a living, mostly for
companies that have just discovered their docs are eight years out of date, and I
keep Field Notes on the side. The entries are about sentences: the ones I cut, the
ones I could not fix, and the small rules I have collected from arguing about them
for eleven years.

Most of it starts as a note typed on my phone on the 14 tram, gets rewritten twice
in a text editor, and ends up here a week later at about half the length.

Field Notes is deliberately small. There are no categories, no comment system, and
no analytics telling me how many people bounced. There is a folder, some Markdown
files, and a build step that turns them into a website.

This site is also the starter example for Overprint, the app I write it in. If you
opened this folder to learn how Overprint works, start with the welcome post and
read forward. The three published posts cover the folder layout, the five fields at
the top of every file, and how drafts behave.

The address is in the footer. I answer, eventually.
